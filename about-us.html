Sandid Contracting LLC is a Dubai-based contracting company specializing in general construction, infrastructure works, and premium interior fit-out solutions. Since our establishment in 2020, we have successfully delivered projects across commercial, industrial, residential, and hospitality sectors with a commitment to quality, safety, and timely execution.

WHO WE ARE

Experienced Professionals.
Trusted Project Partners.

Sandid Contracting LLC was established in Dubai with the vision of providing holistic contracting and fit-out solutions that combine technical expertise, quality workmanship, and professional project management.

Our team delivers comprehensive construction and interior fit-out services, managing every stage from planning and coordination to handover and final handover. We take pride in building long-term client relationships through transparency, accountability, and exceptional service standards.
